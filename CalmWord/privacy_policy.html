Privacy Policy for CalmWord

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 · Last Updated: November 26, 2026

Welcome to CalmWord. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of our players. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use CalmWord on iOS and iPadOS.

1. Information Collection and Storage

A. Local Game Data (On-Device Storage): All your game progress, completed puzzle levels, daily streaks, gold balances, remaining booster counts, best scores, and preferences (sound, haptics, daily reminder time) are stored locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults. We do not operate external user databases or require account registration.

B. Advertising (Google AdMob): CalmWord displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC) to support free gameplay. Google AdMob may process device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV), approximate IP-based location, and diagnostic/crash data. See Google Privacy & Terms.

C. Local Notifications: If you enable the optional Daily Zen Reminder in Settings, notifications are scheduled locally on your device via Apple's UNUserNotificationCenter.

2. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS 14.5 and newer, CalmWord asks for your permission via Apple's ATT framework:

  • Allow: Google AdMob may serve personalized ads.
  • Ask App Not to Track: Google AdMob serves only non-personalized, contextual ads.

You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > CalmWord.

3. Children's Privacy

CalmWord does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

4. Data Security

Your gameplay data stays safely inside your device's sandboxed iOS storage and is protected by standard iOS security.
