Privacy Policy for NumZen

Effective Date: November 28, 2026 • Developer: Adil Biçer (Adensoft) • Bundle ID: com.adil.numzen

Thank you for playing NumZen. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the NumZen mobile application.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

NumZen does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Your game progress—including current level, coin balance, stars, daily puzzle completion history, daily streak, theme preference, sound/haptic settings, and optional daily reminder schedule—is stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

NumZen displays banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process device identifiers, IP address, coarse location, ad interaction events, and performance diagnostics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from apps that use our services

3. In-App Purchases

NumZen offers an optional non-consumable Remove All Ads in-app purchase processed securely through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive, process, or store your payment card or Apple ID billing details.

4. Local Notifications

If you enable the optional Daily Reminder in Settings, NumZen schedules local notifications on your device via Apple's UserNotifications framework. No push notification tokens or personal data are transmitted to external servers.

5. Children's Privacy

NumZen does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us via our GitHub repository: https://github.com/adensoft82/NumZen
