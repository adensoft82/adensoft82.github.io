🐾 Pawdoku Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Welcome to Pawdoku ("we", "our", or "the Game"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of our players. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when you use Pawdoku on iOS.

1. Information Collection and Storage

A. Local Game Data (On-Device Storage)
All your game progress, level completions, streaks, coins, fish treats, cat room items, and user settings (sound, haptics, auto-mark) are stored strictly locally on your device using Apple's standard storage (UserDefaults). We do not operate external player database servers, and no account registration is required to play Pawdoku.

B. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)
Pawdoku displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob (Google LLC) to support free gameplay. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Apple IDFA/IDFV), IP address, and diagnostic information in accordance with their privacy policy: Google Privacy & Terms.

2. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS 14.5 and newer, Pawdoku requests your explicit permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework:

  • If you tap "Allow": Google AdMob may use your advertising identifier to serve ads tailored to your interests.
  • If you tap "Ask App Not to Track": Contextual, non-personalized ads will be shown instead. Your choice does not limit game features or rewards in any way.

You can change your tracking preferences at any time in your iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Pawdoku.

3. Children's Privacy

Pawdoku is a cozy puzzle game suitable for families and all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe any personal information has been inadvertently collected, please reach out so we can promptly address it.

4. Data Security

Because no personal data is transmitted to or stored on external servers, your gameplay data remains safely stored within your own iOS device sandbox.
